# Code of Conduct

LibreScreen adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its full text applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone
represents the project.

## In short

We want taking part in LibreScreen to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

What helps:

- Being welcoming and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Giving and receiving feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assuming good intent, and owning mistakes when they happen.

What is not acceptable:

- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, contact the maintainer privately through
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/renatoasterio), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment,
issue or discussion. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions
and block accounts that do not follow this code.
